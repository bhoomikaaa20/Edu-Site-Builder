Applications

AI in the real world

AI isn't only in research labs. Healthcare teams, financial analysts, and educators are using it every day to make decisions, automate repetitive work, and unlock insights inside their data.

Where AI is used today

The table below summarizes a few well-known applications of AI across three industries — what the AI does, why it matters, and an example you might recognize.

Industry AI Use Case Why It Matters Real-World Example
Healthcare Medical image analysis Detects diseases earlier and more consistently than the human eye alone AI-assisted reading of CT scans and X-rays
Healthcare Drug discovery Shortens the time it takes to identify promising molecules Protein-folding tools predicting molecular structures
Finance Fraud detection Spots unusual transaction patterns in milliseconds Credit card networks blocking suspicious purchases
Finance Algorithmic trading Reacts to market signals faster than any human trader High-frequency trading systems used by hedge funds
Education Personalized learning Adapts difficulty and pace to each student's progress Adaptive math platforms used in K–12 classrooms
Education Automated grading & feedback Frees teachers to focus on mentoring instead of marking Essay-feedback tools used in higher education
Education Conversational tutors Gives students 24/7 access to step-by-step explanations Chat-based study assistants powered by LLMs

Why these examples?

These three industries are useful illustrations because they cover very different goals: life-critical accuracy in healthcare, speed and risk management in finance, and personalization at scale in education. Together they show that AI is less of a single technology and more of a set of tools shaped by the problem you're solving.
