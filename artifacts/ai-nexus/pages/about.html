About

Why this site exists

AI Nexus is a small, focused educational website that explains Artificial Intelligence in plain language. It is built for learners who want a clear starting point, free of jargon, marketing, or hype.

Our purpose

We believe everyone — not just engineers — benefits from understanding how AI works. AI Nexus distills the most important ideas into short, structured pages that you can read in any order and return to later.

Who this site is for

This site is written for four kinds of readers:

  • Students learning AI for school or self-study.
  • Beginners who want a non-technical overview.
  • Researchers looking for a quick refresher on basics.
  • Tech enthusiasts following the broader field.

Why AI matters

Artificial Intelligence is reshaping how we work, learn, and create. From healthcare diagnostics to personal assistants, AI is becoming an invisible utility — like electricity or the internet. Knowing the basics is no longer a specialty; it's becoming a baseline skill.

How to use this site

Start with AI Basics if you're new, then explore Technologies for a deeper look at the engines behind modern AI. Applications shows where AI is being used today, and Comparison contrasts AI with traditional rule-based systems.
